🧠 Your Knowledge Classification System

The chart below is only useful if you classify honestly. A yellow concept is not a failure — it tells you that you need practice rather than more passive studying.

🔴 RED — DON'T UNDERSTAND You cannot explain the concept, don't know how to start, or don't know which method to use.

Action: Review briefly → basic problems → explain it yourself.
🟡 YELLOW — NEED PRACTICE You understand the idea, but make mistakes, need hints, or are too slow.

Action: Practice → check errors → redo → timed practice.
🟢 GREEN — CONFIDENT You can solve unfamiliar problems independently and explain why your method works.

Action: Move on. Revisit before exams.
⚪ NOT ASSESSED You haven't determined your level yet.

Action: Do a few problems or active recall and classify it honestly.
